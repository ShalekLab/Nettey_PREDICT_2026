# Nettey_PREDICT_2026

This is a repository for analysis contributing to the 2026 Nettey et al. Immunity publication resulting from the PREDICT (Precision Diagnostics in Inflammatory Bowel Disease, Cellular Therapy and Transplantation) study. 

This study recruited nearly 80 pediatric study participants recruited at Seattle Children's Hospital and Boston Children's Hospital during workup for suspected IBD. Each participant has a pre-treatment diagnostic endoscopy and has one of three diagnoses assigned: 

*	Inflammatory Bowel Disease
    *   Crohn’s Disease (CD, subgrouped into ileal-only CD and colon-involved CD) 
    *	Ulcerative Colitis (UC)
*	Non-inflammatory Bowel Disease
    * Functional Gastrointestinal Disorders (FGID)

For a proportion of participants there is a follow-up endoscopy, either because their disease was resistant to anti-TNF therapy, or to document potential GI tract healing following therapy. Tissues from each site were separated into epithelial and lamina propria layers before undergoing both 5’ single-cell sequencing of total gene expression (GEX) and the T cell receptor genes (VDJ). Additional data analysis included immunohistory chemistry (IHC) and autoantigen analysis with the HuProt microarray from CDI Labs. Participant SNPs were assessed using the IDT xGen v1 whole exome sequencing platform to assess HLA allele usage.
